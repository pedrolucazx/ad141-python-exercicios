The cyclone begins with a gentle whisper, a subtle shift in the air that goes unnoticed. As the cyclone gains strength, the winds howl across the plains. After the cyclone, the community comes together to rebuild. A cyclone is a powerful natural phenomenon that cannot be controlled. The damage from a cyclone can be devastating.
